# MSC-D-001 v1 — review

Status: `REFINE`, not approved.

## What works

- Delta silhouette and central triangular opening are visible.
- Blue/green ribbon material is recognizably linked to Dual Delta.
- No fake data, text, crypto coin, robot chassis, or gaming UI.

## Blocking issues

1. Raised green ribbon tip reads as an elephant trunk/hand and breaks the pure `Δ` silhouette.
2. Large glossy oval eyes push the character toward children's animation.
3. The asymmetric gesture may be hard to reproduce consistently across turnaround poses.

## Refinement brief

Keep the ribbon construction and color split. Close the outer Delta silhouette, remove the raised appendage, reduce the eyes into narrow integrated light apertures, and express life through a subtle forward lean plus one controlled fold.
