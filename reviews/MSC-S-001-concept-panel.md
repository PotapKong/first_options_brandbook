# MSC-S-001 — Strike concept panel

## Decision

Selected concept: **«Калибр» — fixed measure**.

| Concept | Character designer | Brand strategist | Options educator | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Калибр | 9 | 9 | 9 | selected |
| Порог | 8 | 8 | 8 | reserve for educational scenes |
| Камертон | 8 | 7 | 6 | rejected |

## 1. Калибр — selected

**Form:** asymmetric C-shaped architectural gauge with a large inner opening and one fixed green reference mark. Negative space may subtly echo `1`.

**Character:** calm, precise, concise. It does not predict direction; it defines the contract condition.

**Motion:** one controlled calibration click, then stability while Delta changes angle or moves above/below the reference mark.

**Material:** matte deep-navy ceramic, satin cobalt-blue inner edge, thin emerald-green indicator.

**Meaning:** a pre-set measure against which the underlying price is compared. Strike does not chase the market.

**Risks:** magnet, horseshoe, clamp, letter C. Avoid literal caliper anatomy and use asymmetric soft architectural geometry.

## 2. Порог — reserve

Strong educational metaphor for crossing below/above strike and OTM/ITM. Use later as an environment or scene object, not the mascot. Never imply that crossing strike automatically means profit; premium and expiry still matter.

## 3. Камертон — rejected

The fixed-reference idea is elegant, but the silhouette risks bull horns, slingshot, weapon and music-brand associations. Too much explanation is required.

## Production brief

Premium living 3D Strike mascot for an options-education brand. Compact asymmetric C-shaped gauge body with a strong silhouette, large negative space and one fixed horizontal reference mark representing the exercise price. Matte deep-navy ceramic, satin cobalt-blue inner edge, thin emerald-green indicator. Minimal expression, calm precise character, no literal face and no generic robot anatomy. Visually paired with the flexible Delta mascot: Strike remains stable while Delta changes. Three-quarter view, soft studio light, navy background, square 1:1, circular-crop safe, readable at 64 px. No text or numbers. Exclude crypto, gaming, casino, bull/bear, coins, rockets, targets, weapons, candlesticks, magnet, horseshoe, clamp and childish animation.
