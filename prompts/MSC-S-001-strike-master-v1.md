# MSC-S-001 — Strike master · production prompt draft

Create a premium living 3D mascot named Strike for the adult Russian options-education brand «Первый опционный». Use the approved Dual Delta logo and the final Delta mascot as strict references for palette, material, lighting, scale, and character restraint.

Strike is a **fixed measure**, not a target. Build a compact asymmetric C-shaped architectural gauge with a strong ownable silhouette and a large inner negative-space opening. Inside the opening place one clearly fixed horizontal reference mark representing the exercise price. The mark is part of the body and never floats or moves. Negative space may subtly echo the numeral `1`, but must not become a generic letter C.

Character: calm, precise, reliable, concise. Strike does not predict market direction and does not chase Delta; it sets the contract condition. Give it minimal integrated expression through two tiny calm light apertures or a single restrained indicator surface, without a literal cartoon face. No mouth and no generic robot anatomy.

Motion logic frozen into the pose: the body feels locked after one precise calibration click. Stable vertical posture, slight three-quarter turn, firm floor contact. It should visually complement flexible triangular Delta: Strike is compact and stable; Delta is open and adaptive.

Material: matte deep-navy ceramic body, satin cobalt-blue inner edge, one thin emerald-green reference indicator. Match Dual Delta's rounded precision, controlled folds and restrained rim light. Dark system-navy #050B24 studio background, subtle floor shadow, generous clear space, square 1:1, safe for circular crop and readable at 64 px.

No text, numbers, charts, interface or props. Do not create a magnet, horseshoe, clamp, caliper, letter C logo, target, crosshair, portal, road barrier, shield, weapon, tuning fork, bull horns, candlestick, coin, crypto token, gaming badge, robot, Pixar/Disney character, toy, gold object or watermark.
