# C — Sculpted Option Orbit + One

Create one unmistakable warm-white numeral `1` framed by two separate partner-color arcs. Use distinctive asymmetric sculpted matte-satin geometry: cobalt-blue lower arc sweeps under the numeral; vivid-green upper arc sweeps over it; the arcs use different radii, tapered cut endpoints and overlap in depth at one controlled point without forming a full ring. The numeral stays dominant and forward. Dark navy background, restrained 3D, circular-crop safe, readable at 40 px.
