# MSC-D-001 — Delta mascot master · prompt v1

Create a premium living 3D mascot for the Russian options education brand «Первый опционный», using the attached approved Dual Delta logo as the strict material, color, geometry, and lighting reference.

## Character

The mascot itself must have the unmistakable silhouette of the mathematical Greek capital Delta symbol `Δ`: a strong triangular outer body with a large clean triangular negative-space opening. Build the body from one broad folded ribbon with the same smooth rounded corners, controlled hard folds, matte satin surface, and precise blue/green edge treatment as the reference logo.

This is Delta as a living educational guide: attentive, adaptive, curious, quick to notice change. Give it two small expressive eyes integrated subtly into the upper inner face of the ribbon, not a pasted cartoon face. A slight forward lean and one raised ribbon-tip gesture should make it feel alive and alert. If compact limbs are needed, integrate them structurally into the ribbon folds; do not attach generic arms, gloves, boots, or a humanoid robot body.

## Color logic

Deep electric blue is dominant on the moving left/lower face; bright partner green appears as a substantial secondary folded face and rim, not a tiny decorative accent. Both colors must be visible and balanced enough to belong to Dual Delta.

## Composition

Single full-body character, centered, neutral hero pose, three-quarter view, complete silhouette visible, generous clear space, square composition. Dark system-navy studio background `#050B24`, subtle floor contact shadow, restrained blue/green rim light. No props, no text, no letters besides the body being `Δ`, no charts, no interface.

## Finish

High-end brand character design, clean premium 3D product rendering, tactile matte ribbon material, precise edges, minimal micro-detail, visually reproducible across future poses. Sophisticated for adult financial education, approachable but not childish.

## Hard rejection constraints

Do not make a Pixar/Disney character, generic robot, crypto coin, esports badge, superhero, monster, toy, letter A, pyramid, warning sign, road sign, play button, shield, arrow, candlestick, or neon cyberpunk object. No mouth with teeth, no oversized cartoon eyes, no eyebrows floating above the form, no gloves, no sneakers, no helmet, no cape, no weapon, no money, no gold, no fake financial data, no watermark, no readable text.
